# 2026 Project Review — Client / Manager Questionnaire

For the managers who commissioned and received the regional priorities report.
Responses will be attributed by name in the review document.

**Name:**
**Role / relationship to the project:**

---

## 1. The brief

1. Did the finished report meet what you originally asked for? If not, where did it
   fall short or exceed expectations?
2. Was the priority structure (six chapters: economy, transport, place, skills,
   environment, child poverty) the right framing, or would you organise it differently
   next time?

## 2. Visibility during the project

3. Did you have adequate visibility into progress while the project was running, or did
   it feel opaque until the report was close to done?
4. Were you consulted at the right points, or would you have wanted earlier/more
   frequent input on content or direction?
5. If there were delays or changes in scope, were you given enough notice and
   explanation?

## 3. Quality and usefulness

6. Is the report in a format/style that's usable for your purposes (briefings, external
   publication, board reporting, etc.)?
7. Are the indicators and the way progress is shown (the summary tables, sparklines,
   direction-of-travel indicators) meaningful and trustworthy to you?
8. Is there anything in the report you don't trust, don't understand, or think is
   missing?

## 4. Process

9. From your side, did the resourcing (one analyst per priority area plus a
   coordinator) seem right for the scope of the work?
10. Would you commission this the same way again next year, or would you want a
    different approach (different team shape, different timeline, different level of
    your own involvement)?

## 5. Looking forward

11. What's the single biggest improvement you'd want to see in next year's version?
12. Anything else you'd like to flag?
